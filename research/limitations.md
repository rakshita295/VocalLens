# Potential Issues to Investigate

## Overview
Potential limitations and areas to investigate during development and empirical evaluation of the CVSM implementation:

- Robustness across diverse vocal styles, genres, and singing techniques
- Impact of varying instrumental accompaniment and complex background musical mixtures
- Performance sensitivity across different audio qualities, recording conditions, and sample rates
- Generalization across language and vocal timbre variations
- Latency and compute constraints in interactive or real-time inference contexts
